## 💡 About the Project

RecruitX is a student-focused job information platform that brings together job opportunities from top companies into one clean, easy-to-use interface.

Instead of hunting across multiple websites, students can visit **RecruitX** to find, search, and explore job listings — all in one place.

Every listing includes:
- Role
- Company
- Location
- Salary range
- Eligibility criteria
- Description
- Last date to apply

---

## ❗ The Problem We're Solving

Students today struggle to keep track of job opportunities:

- 😓 Jobs are scattered across multiple platforms  
- 📋 Important details are often missing  
- ⏰ Students miss deadlines  
- 🔍 No centralized student-focused platform  
- 😵 Job search becomes overwhelming
